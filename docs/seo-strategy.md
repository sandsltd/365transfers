### 5.1 Current Strengths

#### Schema & Structured Data
- **Organization Schema** — name, URL, logo, contact, service type, area served
- **LocalBusiness Schema** — address, geo coords (52.9069, -2.1394), opening hours 00:00-23:59 all 7 days, price range $$
- **TaxiService Schema** — provider links to Organization, covers Stone/Stoke/Staffordshire
- **Service Schemas** — dynamically generated for each service page
- **Breadcrumb Schemas** — present on every page with nested hierarchy
- **Article Schemas** — used on all 6 blog posts
- **FAQ Schemas** — present on homepage and all major service pages

#### Meta Tags & Open Graph
- Comprehensive meta titles, descriptions, keywords on all pages
- Open Graph tags with proper og:title, og:description, og:type, og:locale (en_GB), og:image
- Twitter Card tags (summary_large_image) on all pages
- Canonical URLs explicitly set on every page

#### Technical Infrastructure
- Next.js 14 with App Router (automatic code splitting)
- Dynamic sitemap (28 URLs with proper priority and changefreq)
- Proper robots.txt (allows /, disallows /api/ and /_next/)
- Image optimization (AVIF, WebP via Next.js Image component)
- Responsive design (mobile-first, all breakpoints)
- Web manifest for PWA capabilities

#### Content Coverage
- 11 service pages with standardized structure
- 6 blog posts (December 2025 - January 2026)
- FAQ sections on homepage and every service page
- 10 comprehensive FAQ Q&As on homepage

### 5.2 Issues to Address

#### CRITICAL: Domain Consolidation
**Problem:** Three separate websites splitting authority:
1. taxisstone.co.uk (primary, well-optimized)
2. stokeontrentairporttaxi.co.uk (separate site targeting Stoke airport transfers)
3. 365transfers.net (another separate site)

**Impact:** Link equity, domain authority, and brand signals are diluted across three domains. Google may struggle to determine which is the canonical authority.

**Recommendation:**
- Keep taxisstone.co.uk as the primary domain (strongest local keyword match)
- 301 redirect stokeontrentairporttaxi.co.uk to taxisstone.co.uk/airport-transfers
- 301 redirect 365transfers.net to taxisstone.co.uk
- Consolidate all content and backlinks to the primary domain

#### HIGH: Missing Location Pages
**Problem:** No dedicated pages for surrounding towns/cities:
- No `/taxi-stafford` page
- No `/taxi-stoke-on-trent` page
- No location pages for Newcastle-under-Lyme, Uttoxeter, Eccleshall, etc.

**Recommendation:** Create location-specific landing pages for each major service area with local content, testimonials, and schema markup.

#### HIGH: Missing Key Service Pages
**Problem:** Important services lack dedicated pages:
- No dedicated Alton Towers transfer page (300-500 monthly searches)
- No wheelchair-accessible services page
- No Liverpool Airport transfer page
- No London airports transfer page
- No Keele/Staffordshire University page
- No train station transfers page

#### MEDIUM: No Published Pricing
**Problem:** The main competitor (Stafford & Stone Airport Transfers) publishes fixed prices. 365 Transfers does not.

**Impact:** Customers searching "how much taxi to Manchester airport from Stoke" will find competitors first. Published pricing builds trust and captures pricing-intent searches.

**Recommendation:** Add a pricing page or pricing sections to airport transfer pages with fixed prices.

#### MEDIUM: Blog Content Gaps
**Problem:** Only 6 blog posts exist, and none of the competitors are doing content marketing either — this is a wide-open opportunity.

**Recommendation:** Implement a content calendar targeting high-value informational keywords (see Section 7).

#### LOW-MEDIUM: Analytics & Tracking
**Problem:** No visible Google Analytics, Search Console, or conversion tracking on the site.

**Recommendation:** Implement GA4, Google Search Console, and call tracking to measure SEO performance.

## 10. Wheelchair Accessible Transport — Regulatory & Market Context

### 10.1 Stoke-on-Trent Regulations (2025-2030 Policy)

- Public register maintained of all wheelchair-accessible taxis
- Wheelchairs can be loaded from side or rear
- Suitable restraint must be available for wheelchair occupants
- Ramps must comply with BSI PAS 2012-1 standard
- Slip-resistant steps required for every 300mm rise, treads at least 150mm deep
- Open edges highlighted in contrasting highly visible colour
- **After April 2031:** Only electric and hybrid taxis will be licensed

### 10.2 UK Legal Requirements (Equality Act 2010)

- **Section 165:** Drivers must transport wheelchair users in their wheelchair and provide appropriate assistance
- Refusing to carry a wheelchair user is a **criminal offence** (up to £1,000 fine)
- Cannot charge extra for wheelchair users
- Exemptions only available with medical certificate

### 10.3 Market Opportunity

**Specialist wheelchair transport providers in the area:**
- Cabability (Stoke-based, UK-wide, purpose-built fleet)
- Regency Private Hire (Stoke-on-Trent)
- Magnum Taxis (24/7 wheelchair service)
- Deluxe Travels (wheelchair accessible Stoke)

365 Transfers already has wheelchair-accessible vehicles. The opportunity is to:
1. Create a dedicated wheelchair services page with detailed accessibility information
2. Target SEND school transport contracts
3. Market to care homes and NHS non-emergency patient transport
4. Build reputation as a reliable accessible transport provider
